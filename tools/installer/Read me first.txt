============================================================
  LAZYKICK
  Paste images, keep project notes and auto-import media
  inside After Effects and Premiere Pro
============================================================

WHAT YOU NEED
  - Windows 10 / 11, or macOS
  - Adobe After Effects (CC 2019 or newer) and/or
    Adobe Premiere Pro (CC 2020 or newer)


------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALL
------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Unzip this download anywhere. The Desktop is fine.
  2. Close After Effects and Premiere Pro if they are open.
  3. Windows: double-click  "Install LazyKick.bat"
     macOS:   double-click  "Install LazyKick (macOS).command"
              (if macOS refuses, right-click it and choose Open)
  4. Wait for "Installed", then press a key.
  5. Start the app and open:
       Window > Extensions > LazyKick

  The panel is signed, so nothing else needs installing and
  no Adobe setting has to be changed. Updating from an older
  LazyKick? Just run the installer: your notes and watch bins
  are kept.


------------------------------------------------------------
THE THREE TABS
------------------------------------------------------------
  PASTE IMAGE (top of the panel, always there)
    Copy a screenshot, a picture from a browser, or an image
    file in Explorer / Finder. Click "Paste Image" - or press
    Ctrl+V (Cmd+V) while the panel is focused, outside the
    notes. The image is saved in a "Pasted Images" folder next
    to your project and placed at the playhead:
      After Effects  a new layer at the current time (a guide
                     layer by default, so it never renders)
      Premiere Pro   the lowest video track that is free at the
                     playhead; nothing on the timeline moves
    Paste the same picture twice and the same file is reused.

  NOTES & TASKS
    Notes are saved per project, automatically. "Global" is a
    scratchpad shared by every project. Buttons:
      +          new note tab (double-click a tab to rename it)
      Timecode   inserts the playhead time, e.g. [00:01:24:12]
      Task       inserts a checkbox
      Copy       copies the note as text
      Export     saves a .txt next to the project

  WATCH BINS
    Link a folder on your computer (Downloads, SFX, client
    uploads...) to a bin in the project. "Sync" imports what is
    new; turn on Auto-Sync and new files come in by themselves
    every few seconds - once they have finished copying.
    Files that cannot be imported are shown as "skipped" and
    tried again when they change, or when you click Sync.


------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------
  The panel is not in the Window menu
      Restart After Effects / Premiere Pro. Adobe only looks
      for new panels while the app is starting.

  The panel opens blank
      Windows: run "Fix a blank panel.bat" and restart the app.
      macOS: in Terminal run
        defaults write com.adobe.CSXS.12 PlayerDebugMode 1
      and restart the app.

  "No image in clipboard"
      Copy an actual picture, or an image file (PNG, JPG, GIF,
      BMP, TIFF, WEBP, PSD). Copying a file path as text does
      not count.

  "No free video track at the playhead" (Premiere Pro)
      Every unlocked video track has a clip under the playhead.
      Add an empty video track, or move the playhead. The image
      is already in the project bin.

  A watch folder shows "skipped" files
      Hover the word "skipped" to see which files. They are a
      format the app cannot import, or damaged.


------------------------------------------------------------
UNINSTALL
------------------------------------------------------------
  Windows: double-click  "Uninstall LazyKick.bat"
  macOS:   double-click  "Uninstall LazyKick (macOS).command"
  Both ask before deleting your notes and settings.
